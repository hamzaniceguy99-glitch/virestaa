How we work

Your frames, your climate, your calendar

The same week that calls for supering in one state calls for feeding in another. Generic calendars are the reason new keepers add space too late and treat too early.

Photographs fix the other half of the problem. Beginners describe what they think they saw; a picture of the frame shows eggs, pattern and cells, and the answer usually follows in seconds.

What stays outside our scope is anything veterinary or regulatory. We explain how to monitor and how to read a label, and the decisions and the compliance stay with you.
