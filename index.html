How we work

Coaching, not a bee supplier

Virestaa sells no bees, no equipment and no treatments, and takes nothing from anyone who does. That keeps the advice about your colony rather than about a catalog.

Two things stay with you: checking whether hives are allowed where you live and registering if your state requires it, and following the label on anything you put in a hive. We will remind you, and we will not tell you to ignore either.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

I am allergic to stings. Can I keep bees?

Talk to your doctor first, seriously. Beekeepers get stung. If you have had a systemic reaction, that is a medical conversation before it is a beekeeping one.

Do I need permission to keep bees?

Often yes. Cities, HOAs and states vary, and some require registration. Check before you order bees — we cover how to find the rules, but the responsibility is yours.

Can you tell me what treatment to use?

We can explain monitoring, timing and how to read a label. We are not veterinarians, and some products in the US require a veterinary prescription.

My colony is queenless. Can you fix it by video?

We can help you confirm it and choose between a queen, a frame of eggs or combining. Fast answers matter here, which is why reviews are 24 hours in season.

How many hives should I start with?

Two, if you can. A second colony gives you a comparison and a source of resources when one struggles.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no minimum term. Access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for.
